All figures are simulated data. Seed 42, 1,000 runs per scenario and strategy, loop 0.44 s.
Predictions (made once per case and mode, before the loop): Detection RPT_ONLY sap-rpt-1.6, LEARNING sap-rpt-1.6 + posterior. No SAP-RPT or LLM call inside the loop.
GESIT_RPT_ONLY decides with SAP-RPT alone; GESIT_LEARNING with P(late) = 0.5 x SAP-RPT + 0.5 x reliability posterior (Beta, forgetting 0.97 per receipt).
ALWAYS_CHEAPEST, ALWAYS_FASTEST and KEEP_CURRENT_PO are built from the RPT_ONLY ranked table, as before.
Outcomes: true delivery behaviour of the simulated world (data_gen/world.py), one draw per supplier and promised date per run, shared by all strategies.
Stock-out: realised stock-out days = quantity-weighted average over the parts of an order of max(0, real arrival - stock-out date); real arrival = promised date + delay drawn from the simulated world's true behaviour. Short shipments are not counted.
Cost = purchase cost (kg x NETPR; the revised price when keeping the PO) + stock-out days x Rp150,000,000 per day of downtime.
PREDICTED = the expected cost of that row in the strategy's own ranked table (FR-SEL-2). MEAN DIFF = the strategy minus the other one per run; negative means the strategy costs less. CHEAPER and SAME = share of runs where it costs less, or the same.
EXCLUDE_FLAGGED_SUPPLIERS: True.
